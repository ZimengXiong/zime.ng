    Out-SEOing My Friend on His Own Name for his birthday
                                                                25 nov 2026


   My friend Naman Siddheshwar has a personal website at namans dot me
   (for the sake of not giving his website more backlinks, I have made the
   decision to not include a hyperlink). Crucially, he made the mistake of
   NOT buying namansiddheshwar.com, so god forbid some domain squatter
   comes along and registers that.

   Coincidentally, by 8:33 AM PST on November 24th, 2026, I was in proud
   ownership of namansiddheshwar.com after accidentally submitting a
   digital request of my intent to purchase and register the domain to
   Cloudflare.

   

   ## Turning the Leaf
   -------------------

   If the site was a low-effort landing page with just two paragraphs and a
   meme, Google would never rank it above his actual presence. And so,
   being the nice person I am, I decided to gift Naman Siddheshwar a free
   website redesign.

   Luckily Naman Siddheshwar is a big fan of open source, so it was nice to
   see that I could access the source code behind namans dot me fairly
   easily. This meant I could use and present his actual content on my new
   site.

   So I cloned his repo and rebuilt everything over the course of twelve
   hours (all 37+ pages of projects, write-ups, awards, certificates,
   résumé, videos) into an editorial layout (inspired taken straight from
   ndstudio.gov), making sure to do due diligence with webp-compressed
   images, a dithering load animation over his portrait, and a crisp NS
   monogram favicon.

   

   I think I outdid myself with this one, truly. I have never spent this
   much time on a personal portfolio.

   ## optimizing the engines
   -------------------------

   To prevent our friend GoogleBot from being blocked from carrying out its
   work on our site, we have to lazy mount the paywall DOM until scroll
   hits 60%, that way only users see it (as GoogleBot never scrolls or
   clicks). As mentioned earlier, we also don't initiate the redirect
   unless there is user interaction (to prevent being treated as a redirect
   site during indexing).

   Now that we can be indexed, how do we get to the top?

   Normally, ranking #1 for a unique personal name is trivial because you
   just drop your URL into your socials, but obviously I didn't have access
   to any of his accounts. So here's what I did:

   - every single page has "Naman Siddheshwar" in <title>, canonical URLs,
     sitemap.xml, feed.xml, llms.txt
   - I added his socials to Person.sameAs in JSON-LD across the core pages,
     plus other features in subjectOf, and put visible rel="me" links in
     the footer so Google's visible-link check matches the structured data.
   - I added backlinks from domains I control, e.g. "Featured Clients" on
     alpacawebservices.com and a shoutout on my homepage.
   - I contacted the guy managing a website that he was featured on
     (3rd-4th place search result for his name), and asked him to add a
     backlink to namansiddheshwar.com.

   ## Funness
   ----------

   Once the site actually looked like something you'd pay an agency for, I
   had to make sure he couldn't just enjoy it for free.

   ### turnstiles

   I spun up [REDACTED.com] using the exact html from a real Cloudflare
   Turnstile page (extracted by turning on my VPN and trying sites until
   it popped up, upon which I would save the page's HTML).

   

   But I couldn't add a redirect on page load—that would make it hard for
   GoogleBot (such a cute name) and other crawlers to actually index my
   site—so when you land on namansiddheshwar.com, we wait for a user
   interaction (say, scrolling) and then redirect you to [REDACTED.com].
   After a second of "Performing security verification", the "Verify you
   are human" checkbox pops up.

   ### reCAPTCHA round-robin

   Unlike the real Cloudflare turnstiles (where most of the time, they are
   running reCAPTCHA v2-esque background checking and you never see a
   prompt), clicking the checkbox brings up the iconic reCAPTCHA "Select
   all squares containing" puzzle.

   

   The top 8 squares are a screenshot of my redesign
   (namansiddheshwar.com). The bottom 8 squares are a screenshot of his old
   site (namans dot me).

   ### paywalls

   After putting so much time into this site, I take back what I told
   myself at the beginning, that I would "gift" Naman Siddheshwar this site
   free of charge. Good design takes time and I deserve compensation for
   my work.

   I will let the pictures do the talking

   

   

   ## realism
   ----------

   The redirect was too fast, normal cf turnstiles take a second to load
   in and you see the spinning circle in your browser. I fixed this by

   1. Adding a server-side delay to incoming requests of ~500ms
   2. Add a multi-redirect chain, first going to trust.[REDACTED.com] and
      then turnstile.[REDACTED.com] so it feels like real things are
      happening

   After all this, it finally seemed pretty real

   You can try out the difference yourself: no delay and
   delay+chained redirects

   ## did it work?
   ---------------

   Within hours of submitting to Search Console, namansiddheshwar.com and
   projects.html started showing up in Google's index. And in less than 24
   hours from registration, I woke up to find we ranked #2

   

   And we outrank his own site!

   

   And lastly, even AI overview picked up on this!

   

   To be nice, I added a feature flag to disable all the gimmicks after his
   birthday, so he gets a pretty site with good SEO out of all this.

   If you are reading this after November 26th, sadly you have missed out
   on all the fun, and I will have transferred this domain to him.

   And for a final backlink, happy birthday Naman!
